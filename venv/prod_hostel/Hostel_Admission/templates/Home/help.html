Instructions Page

How to Fill the Form

1. Click the Form Button and select the appropriate year.
2. Fill in all required details and upload necessary documents.
3. Review and submit the form.
4. You can preview the submitted form afterward.

How to View the Lists

1. Navigate to the respective year section.
2. Download provisional and final lists.
3. Repeat for important dates as needed.

Important Notes for Students

  1. You can only fill one form per year in a single browser session.
  2. To view your form, use the same browser where it was submitted.
  3. The Mining Form is identical to the Normal Department form.
